Profitability & success on gold

Verdicts re-derived from the current Model-4 run, then mt5-developer’s source facts. Figures for engines that do fire are recomputed from primary_Stats.csv; figures for disabled strategies are v15-log carry-overs and flagged as such.

The live mix is the right edge for gold — a long-biased trend/momentum core plus short/breakout diversifiers. In the current run the picture has improved versus v15: every engine that fires is net-positive, the former net-loser (S6) has flipped, and the trend-pullback long that v15 flagged as the biggest conceptual gap is now live and is the third-largest contributor.

What the CSV actually proves

Only 7 engines fired (801 positions; net $33,318.24 report / $33,808.41 gross-CSV; PF 1.47; +148.82R). All seven are net-positive. The two v15 problem-children are resolved on their own terms: S6/Failed-Break went from PF 0.78 to PF 2.94, and Pullback-Continuation went from not-instantiated to +$3,792 / PF 2.46. The long side carries 81% of net ($27,466 of $33,808), but the count split is now ~51/49 long/short (405/396) — not the ~75/25 of the v15 read.

Core edge — the long-biased workhorses

  • Pin Bar — KEEP · volume core. The biggest single engine: 408 trades, PF 1.36, +$14,139, +0.127 avgR. It now runs both directions (197 long / 211 short); the long book is the stronger half (197 tr, PF 1.50, +$10,313). Lowest per-trade edge of the core but by far the highest dollar contribution — the marginal high-volume engine.
  • Engulfing — KEEP · workhorse. 126 trades, PF 1.79, +$9,925, +0.263 avgR, 50% WR — the best expectancy of any high-volume engine, long-only (Bearish Engulfing stays blocked). The quality anchor of the book.
  • MA-Cross — KEEP, scale-aware. Strong quality-per-trade (PF 1.70, +0.295 avgR) but only 38 trades / 7.5yr and effectively NY-blocked (1 of 38 fired in NY). Rare and high-value — do not widen it chasing frequency; that historically dilutes the edge.

Short-side keeper — the regime diversifier

  • Bearish Pin Bar (short side of the Pin Bar engine) — KEEP, but the edge has thinned. 211 shorts, PF 1.21, 40.8% WR, +0.078 avgR, +$3,826 — no longer the book’s best per-trade edge (it was, at PF 1.87, in v15; it is now the weakest per-trade contributor). Still NY-blocked (0 of 211 in NY) and immediate-fill (0/211 confirmed), but it now fires in London (87) as well as Asia (124) — the “Asia-only” framing no longer holds. A diversifying short on a bull metal, kept for correlation not for its own expectancy.
  • Rubber-Band Short (Crash) — KEEP as a regime hedge, eyes open. 150 trades, PF 1.26, +$1,360, +29.09R — and 100% of trades are 2021–2023 (2021: 55, 2022: 70, 2023: 25; zero in 2019–20 or 2024–26 — confirmed in the fresh CSV). Its D1 Death-Cross gate is rarely true on a secular-bull metal, so it contributed nothing in the 2024–25 melt-up and won’t in the next leg up. Correctly built as dormant insurance; don’t “fix” its silence.

Resolved cases — the v15 problem-children now earn their keep

  • Pullback Continuation — KEEP · now live. The single biggest change from v15: it was not instantiated then (its cut rested on stale v11 data). In the current build it fires 39 times, PF 2.46, +$3,792, +0.272 avgR, 56% WR — the third-largest contributor and the highest-PF engine at real volume. The v15 “biggest conceptual gap” recommendation has been actioned and validated. (Edge is long-heavy — the 16 longs carry nearly all of it while the 23 shorts roughly break even; the long-only $ and PF split (~+$3,634 / indicative PF ~6.9) is a directional cut not present in the frozen per-engine export — treat as indicative, not a sourced figure.)
  • S6 Failed-Break (long) — KEEP · thin. Flipped from the v15 net-loser (PF 0.78, −1.65R / 37 tr) to a clean winner: PF 2.94, 80% WR, +$519, +0.300 avgR across 10 longs. All 10 now fire immediately (0/10 confirmation) — the confirmation-routing bug the v15 note ranked as fix #1 appears resolved. Verdict upgraded, but n=10 is far too small to lean on; watch, don’t widen.
  • Expansion / IC Breakout — KEEP · proven small. No longer the 4-trade curiosity of v15: 30 trades, PF 1.97, +$1,736, +0.328 avgR (the richest per-trade of any engine), both directions (18 long / 12 short). A real, if modest, contributor.

The ghosts — inert “ON” strategies

S3 Range-Edge Fade, Displacement (standalone), Volatility Breakout, both Liq-Engine modes, File/CSV and the remaining enabled-but-silent plugins again produced zero fills in the current run (confirmed absent from the 801 positions). They cost nothing and contribute nothing — not diversification, just inert. They’re starved by their own gate stacks (detailed in Evaluation 2). Portfolio read

  • Concentration risk. 79% of net ($26,400) comes from just 3 engines — Pin Bar, Engulfing, MA-Cross (572 of 801 fills) — and 81% of net ($27,466) from the long side. These are predominantly H1 trend-continuation, “buy strength / buy the dip-rejection.” If gold shifts from trending-bull to a prolonged high-vol range (not a bear — a range), the long core degrades together and little built for that environment fires. Note the year-2025 dependence: $20,311 of the $33,318 net (61%) came from 2025 alone; 2026-H1 is a small loss (−$218).
  • Redundancy. Engulfing and the long side of Pin Bar are near-kin (both H1 bullish-rejection candles, same regime/bias gates) — treat their combined ~$20.2k net ($9,925 + $10,313) as ~1.3 engines, not 2.
  • Under-weighted. A working range/mean-reversion tool is still the gap — S3 Range-Edge Fade is meant to be this but is starved to zero fills. (The trend-pullback-long gap the v15 read flagged is now closed: Pullback Continuation is live.)
  • Sizing. Don’t add risk — the B+ tier (96 of 801 fills) is the marginal cohort; if anything tighten toward A+/A (505 A+ and 200 A fills carry the book). The EC-v3 tier model already scales correctly.

Implementation-logic quality

How faithfully the code captures each trading concept — separating concept fidelity from code execution. Grades: Solid / Adequate / Weak / Flawed.

Cross-cutting findings

v15 top bug · appears resolved in this run

Confirmation-routing bug — now fixed

The v15 orchestrator decided confirmation from m_enable_confirmation && !(best_is_mr || SHORT), ignoring the signal’s own requiresConfirmation flag — so S6 long ran the confirmation-candle path despite setting it false, delaying the snapback a full bar. In the current run that is no longer observed: S6 fires immediately on 0/10 confirmed, Crash 0/150, Bearish Pin Bar 0/211, while the confirmed longs (Engulfing 126/126, MA-Cross 38/38, Bullish Pin Bar 197/197) still route through confirmation. The v15 fix #1 appears applied. (Inferred from the CSV ConfirmationUsed column, not re-read in source.)

Low severity · determinism

Forming-bar ATR

MA-Cross and the Liq-Engine read atr_buf[0] (forming H1 bar) for SL sizing while candlestick plugins use the closed atr_buf[1]. Usually benign on a new-bar gate, but non-deterministic intrabar and tester-vs-live divergent. Standardize to [1].

Record corrected

No ranking bug · gold point-math clean

The orchestrator discards plugin qualityScore and re-derives a uniform 0/3/5/7/10 tier, so all plugins rank on one scale — S3’s zero-fire is trigger starvation, not suppression (S6 now fires, 10 trades). And XAUUSD+ point math (1pt = $0.01; 50/150pt buffers = 50c/$1.50) is correctly proportional to gold’s vol — no fixed-pip assumptions.

Strategy-by-strategy

  • Bullish Engulfing — SOLID. Closed-bar engulf ≥0.8× prior body + body>0.3×ATR significance filter (kills doji-engulfs on gold’s noisy H1), regime + H4-bias gated, structural SL. Best concept-code match in the system. No repaint.
  • Bullish Pin Bar — SOLID. Clean rejection definition (lower wick>1.5× body, close upper 30%), H1+H4, structural SL. Only nit: near-redundant with Engulfing (a portfolio issue, not a code one).
  • Bearish Pin Bar — SOLID code, thinner edge now. Faithful mirror logic + session gate (not-NY: 0 of 211 shorts fired in NY) + immediate fill (0/211 confirmed). In the current run it fires Asia (124) and London (87) — no longer Asia-only — and its per-trade edge has fallen to PF 1.21 / +0.078 avgR. Clean implementation; keep for diversification, not expectancy.
  • Bullish MA-Cross — SOLID. SMA(10)/SMA(21) cross on closed bars, tightly regime/NY/H4 gated (explains the rarity + high PF). Note: file comment says “EMA” but it’s SMA — cosmetic mislabel, not a bug. Only risk: forming-bar ATR feeds lot size.
  • Rubber-Band Short — ADEQUATE. Concept gold-correct (D1 Death-Cross + H1 overextension fade). Reads D1 EMA at shift 0 and tests live BID — an intentional front-run, no future look-ahead, but fill-model sensitive. Backtest on real-tick modeling only.
  • S6 Failed-Break (long) — ADEQUATE — concept validated in this run. The v15 routing bug (ran delayed) is gone: all 10 now fire immediately (0/10 confirmed) and the engine is net-positive (PF 2.94, 80% WR). The exhaustion gate looks better-behaved — of just 2 losers, both had MFE<0.3R (0.14R, 0.28R), so the “broke and never snapped back” failure mode still exists but is rare. n=10 is too thin to certify; watch.
  • S3 Range-Edge Fade — ADEQUATE design, effectively DEAD. Exactly the range tool gold needs, but the trigger demands a near-impossible simultaneous conjunction (valid box AND outer-edge AND RSI extreme AND sweep+reclaim AND R≥2). Good logic strangled by over-confluence — relax to OR-of-subset to recover a testable sample.
  • Liq-Engine OB-Retest — FLAWED. Genuine contradiction: a bullish OB is invalidated the instant any H1 candle closes below its bottom, yet the retest requires price to dip back into the zone — the triggering dip self-invalidates the zone. Fix with a wick-tolerance on invalidation — but the repo’s broader “SMC zone-fill underperforms on gold” finding means prove it first.
  • Volatility Breakout — ADEQUATE / intentionally dead. Sound Donchian+Keltner+H4-EMA+ADX logic, gated REGIME_VOLATILE-only (“0 trades, intended”). Adding TRENDING made it fire but was reverted off the proven baseline. Fix is the gate, not the logic.
  • Displacement + Liq-Displacement — ADEQUATE / starved + duplicate. Faithful SMC displacement read; bottleneck is liq_score≥2 demanding the sweep land on a D1/W1/strong-SMC level. The two are near-duplicate detectors on the same H1 bar.
  • IC Breakout (Expansion) — ADEQUATE / now proven. Clean two-state momentum machine with a real TP2. The v15 sample problem is gone: 30 trades, PF 1.97, +$1,736, best-in-book +0.328 avgR, both directions. Code read holds; the edge is now real if modest.
  • Pullback Continuation — SOLID / now live. Instantiated in the current build and profitable (39 tr, PF 2.46, +$3,792). It runs confirmed on the long side and immediate on shorts (16 confirmed / 23 not). Verify it isn’t merely re-firing on Engulfing’s bars, but on the numbers it is a genuine trend-pullback contributor.
  • File / CSV signals — SOLID (as a source). Robust engineering: symbol-alias + GMT→EET DST handling, price-sanity, dedup, opportunistic SL/TP synthesis. Correctly excluded from edge analysis.
  • Disabled set: FVG-Mitigation, SFP, Silver Bullet — code is fine, the concept is gold-wrong; correctly off (v15 finding, not re-run here).
