01

Profitability & success on gold

Verdicts anchored to the v15 trade log first, then mt5-developer’s source facts; the superseded older “audit E” window is discounted. No ICT/SMC restatement double-counted.

The live mix is the right edge for gold — a long-biased trend/momentum core plus two tightly-gated short diversifiers. The defects are narrow: one live loser left on, a swarm of inert “ON” strategies that add nothing, and a missing trend-pullback long whose cut rests on stale data.

What the CSV actually proves

Only 7 strategies fired in v15. The R-edges and rank order are stable across both the CSV and STRATEGIES.md (the doc’s larger $-figures are an older window). Every live long is positive; the only net-negative live strategy is S6.

Keep-core — the long edge

  • Bullish Engulfing — KEEP. The workhorse: 274 trades, PF 1.49, +42.78R. Highest total R, healthy expectancy at volume. This is the system. Confidence high.
  • Bullish MA-Cross — KEEP, scale-aware. Best quality-per-trade in the book (PF 2.37, +0.310 avgR, 51% WR) but only 49 trades / 7yr. Rare and high-value — do not widen it chasing frequency; that historically dilutes the edge.
  • Bullish Pin Bar — KEEP. 227 trades, PF 1.27. Lowest per-trade edge of the longs but real and high-volume; the marginal core contributor.

Short-side keepers — the diversifier

  • Bearish Pin Bar — KEEP. PF 1.87, 51.1% WR, +0.246 avgR — the single best per-trade edge of any live strategy, and a short on a bull metal. The Asia-only / NY-blocked window (~GMT 23:00–08:00, immediate fill, no confirmation) confines shorts to thin Asian liquidity where gold’s bid is weakest. The most under-appreciated strategy relative to its dollar rank.
  • Rubber-Band Short — KEEP as a regime hedge, eyes open. PF 1.32, +13.35R — but 100% of trades are 2021–2023. Its D1 Death-Cross gate is rarely true on a secular-bull metal, so it contributed nothing in 2024–25 and won’t in the next leg up. Correctly built as dormant insurance; don’t “fix” its silence.

Cut-or-prove

  • S6 Failed-Break (long) — LEAN CUT. The only live net-loser (PF 0.78, −1.65R / 37 tr). Loser anatomy (confirmed live): 14/21 losers had MFE<0.3R — the level broke and never snapped back, i.e. the filter is admitting continuation setups, not catching them. Compounded by a confirmation-routing bug that delays the intended fast snapback entry by a full bar (see Evaluation 2). Fix routing + tighten the exhaustion gate and re-test, or disable. Kill if PF<1.0 after the fix.
  • IC Breakout — UNPROVEN. n=4, “PF ∞” is noise. Leave on for optionality; not part of the edge.

The ghosts — inert “ON” strategies

S3 Range-Edge Fade, Displacement (standalone), Volatility Breakout, both Liq-Engine modes, Range Box, False-Breakout Fade, Session Breakout, all Session-Engine modes are ON but fired 0 times. They cost nothing and contribute nothing — not diversification, just inert. They’re starved by their own gate stacks (detailed in Evaluation 2).

Were the disabled strategies correctly cut?

Correctly disabled (high confidence): Bearish Engulfing (−35.3R / 660 tr), S6 Short (−8.9R), Bearish MA-Cross (PF 0.59), FVG-Mitigation (PF 0.56–0.61 — “FVGs on gold H1 behave as momentum, not imbalance to fill”, a genuine gold-specific finding), SFP (0% WR), Silver Bullet / London Breakout / NY Continuation (0% WR / −2.1R — killzone concepts have no edge in this H1 implementation), Panic Momentum (PF 0.47 — gold mean-reverts on velocity spikes, which is exactly why the Rubber-Band fade works and momentum-chase doesn’t).

  • Pullback Continuation — RE-TEST (highest value). The −0.5R / 38-trade verdict is proven stale: it matches the v11 build exactly, measured before v18’s confirmation-routing / EC-v3 / SMC changes. A trend-pullback long is the single biggest conceptual gap in the live mix.
  • Bearish Engulfing — RE-TEST to settle the conflict. The −35.3R headline vs a +$1,062 reading in another era is the largest unexplained cross-source sign-flip. One clean v18 run; kill-criterion stays strict.
  • BB Mean-Reversion / Compression BO — low-priority re-tests only; thin, conflicting samples (n=10), and band-fading on gold is genuinely fragile.

Portfolio read

  • Concentration risk. ~90% of profit comes from 3 long patterns that are all H1, all trend-continuation, all “buy strength / buy the dip-rejection.” If gold shifts from trending-bull to a prolonged high-vol range (not a bear — a range), all three degrade together and nothing built for that environment fires.
  • Redundancy. Bullish Engulfing and Bullish Pin Bar are near-kin (both H1 bullish-rejection candles, same regime/bias gates) — treat their combined +65R as ~1.3 strategies, not 2.
  • Under-weighted. (a) a working range/mean-reversion tool — S3 and Range Box are meant to be this but are starved to zero; (b) a trend-pullback long — arguably gold’s highest-probability bull setup, currently disabled on stale data.
  • Sizing. Don’t add risk — the B+ tier is barely breakeven, so if anything tighten toward A+/A. The EC-v3 tier model already scales correctly.
02

Implementation-logic quality

How faithfully the code captures each trading concept — separating concept fidelity from code execution. Grades: Solid / Adequate / Weak / Flawed.

Cross-cutting findings

System-wide · highest leverage

Confirmation-routing bug

The orchestrator decides confirmation from m_enable_confirmation && !(best_is_mr || SHORT) and ignores the signal’s own requiresConfirmation flag. Verified: S6 long sets it false yet ran the confirmation-candle path on 37/37 trades — the intended immediate mean-reversion snapback is delayed a full bar. The single highest-value code fix.

Low severity · determinism

Forming-bar ATR

MA-Cross and the Liq-Engine read atr_buf[0] (forming H1 bar) for SL sizing while candlestick plugins use the closed atr_buf[1]. Usually benign on a new-bar gate, but non-deterministic intrabar and tester-vs-live divergent. Standardize to [1].

Record corrected

No ranking bug · gold point-math clean

The orchestrator discards plugin qualityScore and re-derives a uniform 0/3/5/7/10 tier, so all plugins rank on one scale — S3/S6 zero-fire is trigger starvation, not suppression. And XAUUSD+ point math (1pt = $0.01; 50/150pt buffers = 50c/$1.50) is correctly proportional to gold’s vol — no fixed-pip assumptions.

Strategy-by-strategy

  • Bullish Engulfing — SOLID. Closed-bar engulf ≥0.8× prior body + body>0.3×ATR significance filter (kills doji-engulfs on gold’s noisy H1), regime + H4-bias gated, structural SL. Best concept-code match in the system. No repaint.
  • Bullish Pin Bar — SOLID. Clean rejection definition (lower wick>1.5× body, close upper 30%), H1+H4, structural SL. Only nit: near-redundant with Engulfing (a portfolio issue, not a code one).
  • Bearish Pin Bar — SOLID — smartest-gated in the book. Faithful mirror logic + double session gate (Asia-only AND not-NY) + immediate fill (0/94 confirmed) — a model gold-aware short implementation.
  • Bullish MA-Cross — SOLID. SMA(10)/SMA(21) cross on closed bars, tightly regime/NY/H4 gated (explains the rarity + high PF). Note: file comment says “EMA” but it’s SMA — cosmetic mislabel, not a bug. Only risk: forming-bar ATR feeds lot size.
  • Rubber-Band Short — ADEQUATE. Concept gold-correct (D1 Death-Cross + H1 overextension fade). Reads D1 EMA at shift 0 and tests live BID — an intentional front-run, no future look-ahead, but fill-model sensitive. Backtest on real-tick modeling only.
  • S6 Failed-Break (long) — WEAK — strong concept, broken execution. Victim of the routing bug (runs delayed) and the snapback-not-exhausted gate (≤1.0×ATR_M15) is too loose, admitting continuation losers. The clearest fix-then-prove case.
  • S3 Range-Edge Fade — ADEQUATE design, effectively DEAD. Exactly the range tool gold needs, but the trigger demands a near-impossible simultaneous conjunction (valid box AND outer-edge AND RSI extreme AND sweep+reclaim AND R≥2). Good logic strangled by over-confluence — relax to OR-of-subset to recover a testable sample.
  • Liq-Engine OB-Retest — FLAWED. Genuine contradiction: a bullish OB is invalidated the instant any H1 candle closes below its bottom, yet the retest requires price to dip back into the zone — the triggering dip self-invalidates the zone. Fix with a wick-tolerance on invalidation — but the repo’s broader “SMC zone-fill underperforms on gold” finding means prove it first.
  • Volatility Breakout — ADEQUATE / intentionally dead. Sound Donchian+Keltner+H4-EMA+ADX logic, gated REGIME_VOLATILE-only (“0 trades, intended”). Adding TRENDING made it fire but was reverted off the proven baseline. Fix is the gate, not the logic.
  • Displacement + Liq-Displacement — ADEQUATE / starved + duplicate. Faithful SMC displacement read; bottleneck is liq_score≥2 demanding the sweep land on a D1/W1/strong-SMC level. The two are near-duplicate detectors on the same H1 bar.
  • IC Breakout — ADEQUATE / unproven. Clean two-state momentum machine with a real TP2; only 4 trades — sample is the problem, not the code.
  • File / CSV signals — SOLID (as a source). Robust engineering: symbol-alias + GMT→EET DST handling, price-sanity, dedup, opportunistic SL/TP synthesis. Correctly excluded from edge analysis.
  • Disabled set: FVG-Mitigation, SFP, Silver Bullet — code is fine, the concept is gold-wrong; correctly off. Pullback Continuation isn’t instantiated in v18, so its quality on the current core is unknown — worth re-instantiating to grade.
