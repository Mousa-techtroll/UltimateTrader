## What changed
- **File touched (verified against current source):**
  - `Include/Validation/CSetupEvaluator.mqh` `EvaluateSetupQuality()` lines ~116–177.
    - **Before:** Factor-1 trend alignment added straight into the running `points` total (which already included the bear-regime SHORT +2 boost at 110–114 and was about to receive the pattern/H4 bonus). The CHoCH branch then did `int trend_points = points;` (snapshot of the WHOLE total, NOT the isolated trend subtotal) and an EXCLUSIVE overwrite `points = choch_points;` when `choch_points(2) > trend_points`. Because `trend_points` already carried the +2 bear boost (and pattern/H4 bonus), the `2 > trend_points` test was almost never true → the CHoCH branch was effectively unreachable; and on the rare time it did fire, the absolute overwrite DESTROYED the bear +2 boost and the pattern/H4 bonus.
    - **After (the spec fix):** the true Factor-1 trend alignment (0–3) is accumulated into its OWN local `int trend_alignment` (NOT into `points`). The bear +2 boost (110–114) and the pattern/H4 bonus (143–146) now live in `points` independently. The CHoCH branch computes `int choch_points` (2 when CHoCH-aligned, else 0) and at the end of the block applies `points += MathMax(trend_alignment, choch_points);` — i.e. the EXCLUSIVE max is taken ONLY between trend_alignment and choch_points (the intended "whichever is higher wins"), and added on top of the preserved rest.
- **Diff summary:** isolate the real 0–3 trend_alignment subtotal; `points = rest + MathMax(trend_alignment, choch_points)`. Preserves the bear +2 SHORT boost + pattern/H4 bonus, and makes the CHoCH branch reachable. NO math axis added/removed.
- **Logic-cut check:** NO logic cut. The "CHoCH exclusive-with-trend-alignment" semantic is preserved exactly (max of the two), just applied to the correctly-isolated subtotal instead of the bloated total. All other factors (bear boost, pattern/H4 bonus, RSI, regime, macro, Factor-4 pattern, CI) untouched.
- **+2 cap-at-10 confirmed still after the block:** the `if(points > 10) points = 10;` clamp at lines 307–308 runs AFTER the entire scoring cascade (Factor 1A CHoCH at ~177, Factor 1.5 RSI, Factor 2–5) and BEFORE the tier thresholds (311–314). B-run decoded tier distribution shows only the 4 defined tiers (`SETUP_A_PLUS / SETUP_A / SETUP_B_PLUS`, no SETUP_B reached, no spurious super-tier) → cap intact.
