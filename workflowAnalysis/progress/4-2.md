## stok sign-off
- **SIGNED — verdict: ACCEPT-WITH-FOLLOWUP** (binding). stok verified the mechanism IN SOURCE (not on assertion) and ruled correct on all three sub-questions:
  - **(1a) Scale-LOT-down-not-SL = the only defensible lever.** SL is the structural thesis-invalidation level; widening it to satisfy a portfolio ceiling would corrupt 1R and every per-tier expectancy + the EC v3 controller that depend on it. Under a fixed structural SL, dollar risk is linear in lots → halving lot halves the exposure contribution with zero R distortion (matches the repo's own risk$ = lots·SL-ticks·tick-value identity; verified CTradeOrchestrator:576–601, SL never referenced). Hard-reject when headroom < min-lot is correct (forcing min-lot would breach the ceiling).
  - **(1b) Hand-floor to lot_step (not NormalizeLots) = correct + the subtle part.** NormalizeLots does MathMax-up to min-lot → would round a headroom-fair 0.006 lot UP to 0.01 and re-breach the cap. `MathFloor(scaled/step)*step` guarantees realized lot ≤ headroom-fair → cap can only undershoot, never breach by rounding; then the `<min_lot`→hard-reject check (no snap-up). Correct conservative direction.
  - **(1c) Stop-distance risk-$ measure + equity denominator = dimensionally correct.** The whole EA is R-based / stop-defined risk (tiers are risk-to-stop %, not notional); a 5% "total exposure" ceiling can only sensibly mean 5% of capital at risk if all open stops hit. Notional/margin would be a category error + redundant with margin checks. Reusing the EXISTING CalculatePositionRiskDollars makes the aggregate cap speak the SAME risk language as per-position sizing (no new unvalidated model). Equity (not balance) is correct for a LIVE open-risk concept. **Caveat (not a defect):** the helper sums ORIGINAL stop-distance risk, so trailed/breakeven positions are over-stated → conservative (fail-safe) ceiling that caps slightly early, never late. Right bias; do not change.
  - **(2) Near-flat net + maxDD wiggle = ACCEPTABLE.** +0.09pp/+$42 maxDD is the deterministic-equity ripple off the single 2021-01-04 rescale (a −$214 loser became −$111, lifting equity → ±0.01-lot re-sizing downstream), a path artifact of one 7.5-yr-old trade, not a regression. The fix's value is fat-tail breach elimination (5.84%, audit ~10% → ~5.0%), judged by whether the backstop holds under stress, not by P&L on a path where it barely engaged. Plan's net-drop kill-criterion NOT tripped (net +0.21%).
  - **(3) Config = sound as a backstop; named optimization follow-up, NOT a loop** (see below).
