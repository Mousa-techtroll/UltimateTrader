## Why (rationale)
- Live-only netting correctness: on a netting account same-symbol positions merge into one ticket; if the direct `ResultOrder`/`ResultDeal` binding fails, the old first-match symbol+magic loop could adopt a PRE-EXISTING position (opened before our send) as if it were our fill — corrupting the position-to-ticket binding (wrong entry/SL/lot basis). The discriminator only adopts a position that opened at/after our send with the requested volume; otherwise it defers to the history-deal check rather than bind a stale position. In the HEDGING Strategy Tester this branch is gated off and the fallback is never reached, so the change is provably byte-identical — the fix's benefit is realized only against a live netting broker.
