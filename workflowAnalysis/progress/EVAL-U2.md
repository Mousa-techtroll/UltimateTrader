## Send-path / netting-safety verdict
The live send path is **structurally sound and correctly retcode-first**: `ExecuteSignal` sizes lots and derives/STOPS_LEVEL-clamps SL/TP upstream, then `ExecuteTradeWithRetries` stamps server time before each attempt, fires a plain `CTrade.Buy/Sell` market order (no risky raw `OrderSend`), and classifies failures on `m_trade.ResultRetcode()` first with `GetLastError()` only as the retcode==0 fallback — retries are capped (3 attempts), backoff is exponential and 30s-capped and skipped in the tester, and the harmful retcodes (NO_MONEY/INVALID_STOPS/etc.) correctly do NOT retry. Filling mode is left to CTrade's auto-derivation, which is genuinely safe on the MARKET-execution gold symbol the EA targets (the constructor also sets margin mode), so the missing `SetTypeFilling`/`SetMarginMode` on `g_trade` is not a live defect. The netting-fallback ticket binding is the one real correctness concern: it deliberately refuses to adopt a same-symbol position whose volume differs from the requested lot by more than one step, which collides with the executor's own policy of ACCEPTING partial fills (EXE-01) — on a partial fill under netting, `PositionSelectByTicket` failing would leave the real position unbound by the volume-matched fallback (it would then lean on the looser history-deal check). No path can open a wrong-direction or oversized live trade from this unit; the residual risks are (a) partial-fill ticket desync on netting (HIGH), and (b) a cluster of **inert/dead execution telemetry** — `CheckSlippage` is never called so per-session slippage learning and the `InpMaxSlippagePoints` cap do nothing (EXE-02/EXE-05), and the forming-bar tick-volume leg can spuriously trip the session-quality entry gate at bar-open (EXE-08). The executor's own lot-sizing, margin validation, and `TradeData`/`CTradeUtils` are all dead in the live path (orchestrator owns sizing); only `GetSafeSL/GetSafeTP` (retry-only) and the shock/session-quality helpers are live secondary surfaces.
