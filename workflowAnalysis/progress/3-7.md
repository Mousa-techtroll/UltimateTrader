### 1. KEEP or CUT Trend-Continuation? → **KEEP-BUT-TINY** (engine stays available, OFF-by-default, low-frequency, NOT force-enabled)

**One-line reason:** It PASSES the kill-criterion cleanly and on the RIGHT side of the metric
(PF 20.81 / avg-R +0.312 full AND PF 14.98 / avg-R +0.236 OOS — positive expectancy on its own
entry/SL/TP, long-biased exactly as gold's structural uptrend rewards), so there is no basis to CUT it —
but n=13 over 8 years (~1.6/yr, 11/13 in 2025, five of eight years empty) is **too thin to CERTIFY ON**,
so it stays available-but-OFF-by-default at a low-frequency posture, not promoted into the production
default set.

**Why not full KEEP-ON:** The expectancy is real and correctly-signed, but the sample is statistically
underpowered and **time-concentrated, not time-distributed**. 11 of 13 fills in a single year (2025) and
five empty years means the +0.312R is an estimate over essentially ONE regime sample, not eight. PF 20.81
is the arithmetic of "12 small winners, ~1 tiny loser, $12.75 maxDD" — it is a *clean* number, not a
*robust* one; one bad cluster would move it hard. The engine is the textbook-correct play for gold
(buy discount pullbacks in a confirmed HTF uptrend, OB-retest dominant), so the prior is KEEP — but
certification onto the production risk budget needs more independent samples than this.

**Why not CUT:** Cutting would violate the Iter-3 kill-criterion in reverse — the criterion is "PF<1.0 OR
avgR<0 over full AND OOS → stays OFF"; this engine is the opposite of that on every axis, full and OOS.
There is no measured loss here. CUT is reserved for negative/zero-edge engines (RangeReversion avg-R
−0.688, SFP 0% WR). TrendCont is not that. We keep the option and let it accrue evidence.

**Posture (binding):** `InpEnableEngineTrend=false` STAYS in the production default (engine OFF-by-default,
production byte-identity preserved — already proven through Phases 3.1/3.4/3.5). The engine remains
*available* and *validated-positive on its own numbers*. It is NOT force-enabled into production and NOT
given a shared-tier risk multiplier (the 2.4-GATE verdict already established the scorer mis-ranks; a kept
engine, if ever turned on, trades on its OWN entry/SL/TP + per-engine risk, not the shared tier).

**What would RAISE confidence to a full KEEP-ON (the path off "tiny"):**
1. **Trade count / time-distribution** — get n up toward ≥30–50 with fills spread across ≥4 distinct
   years/regimes, not concentrated in 2025. The single biggest weakness is concentration, not sign.
2. **A structural relaxation to lift frequency WITHOUT degrading avg-R** — the engine is starved
   (~1.6 fills/yr). Candidate relaxations to A/B *individually* on avg-R (each must hold PF>1.0 AND
   avg-R>0 full+OOS or it reverts): (a) widen the OB-retest mitigation tolerance / accept H4 OBs, since
   OB-Retest is the workhorse (10/13, +0.127R); (b) loosen `IsUptrendActive()` from "H4 OR D1 bullish AND
   price>MA200" to also admit clean H1-internal-uptrend pullbacks while D1 is bullish; (c) re-examine why
   Displacement fired 0 times — a dead sub-mode is a frequency leak. Note the composed-Pullback mode,
   though only n=2, carried +1.18 avg-R — worth measuring whether relaxing toward more Pullback fills
   helps or dilutes.
3. **Live-forward / walk-forward confirmation** — because the edge is concentrated in the most recent
   year, a forward sample (paper or micro-size) in 2026+ would be disproportionately informative; it
   tests whether 2025 was the regime or the fluke.

Until (1) or (2) materially lifts n with the sign intact, "keep-but-tiny / OFF-by-default" is the honest
posture. Do NOT relax filters merely to chase count — every relaxation re-runs the kill-criterion on its
OWN isolated avg-R, full+OOS, and reverts if it does not strictly hold.
