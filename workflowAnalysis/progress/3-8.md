### Why this FALSIFIES the freshness-starves-Rubber-Band hypothesis
- **MODE_RUBBER_BAND produced ZERO scored candidates across 2019–2026.** It is gated UPSTREAM of the
  scorer by its own sub-detector trigger + the context gate `(IsBearRegimeActive() || IsRubberBandSignal())`
  at CReversalSweepEngine.mqh:392-393. The scorer (and therefore `InpBOSFreshnessBars` /
  `InpSpineMinConfluence`) is **never reached** for Rubber-Band. No freshness/spine value can manufacture
  a candidate the sub-detector never emitted. The death-cross short simply **did not trigger** on gold's
  2019–2026 regime (its bear-regime precondition rarely opens on a structural uptrend). It is NOT starved
  by the floors — it is starved by its own (correct, regime-appropriate) bear gate.
- **`InpSpineMinConfluence` is NOT binding on any reject here.** All 243 SETUP_NONE rows were SCORED
  (passed the L3 hard gate) — they did not fail the spine. Lowering 25 → lower cannot help and would
  pointlessly approach the validator hard-reject floor (40) discipline for zero surfaced trades.
- **Only S6 (MODE_FAILED_BREAK) is genuinely in the freshness causal path:** 9 candidates, all
  RawScore 5 / ax4=0, all SETUP_NONE. A LONGER freshness window (8→12) could let a stale-but-aligned BOS
