## Problem (verified at source, HEAD e27b903)
`g_session_quality_factor` (UltimateTrader.mq5:359, the live position-size risk multiplier) was modified by:
- the SHOCK path (`*= (1.0 - shock.shock_intensity*0.5)`) — multiplicative, COMPOUNDS;
- the SESSION-QUALITY path (`= session_quality`) — overwrites;

and was NEVER reset per bar. The only reset to 1.0 was the SQ-gate's `else` branch (line 1766), which fires ONLY when `InpEnableSessionQualityGate==true` AND quality is good AND `!shock_blocked`. So on any bar where the SQ gate was disabled, or shock_blocked short-circuited it, or the SQ branch did not hit the `else`, a prior reduction LEAKED forward and (for the shock path) COMPOUNDED across bars. After Phase 4.6 the shock gate ACTIVELY FIRES in the tester (2308 EXTREME + 1259 moderate events), so this factor is live.

## VALIDATION (b) floor binds
- On EXECUTED signals: minimum combined factor observed in B = **0.51**; raw `shock*sq < 0.25` count = **0 of 12**. The floor (`InpMinSessionRiskFactor=0.25`) did NOT bind on any trade in this run.
