### BINDING RULING — concern #1 (axis-4 / axis-5 reuse of the gated `structure_shift`)
**RULING: KEEP the freshness+direction-gated `structure_shift` flowing into axis 4 (sweep+inducement, +1) AND axis 5 (confirmation candle, +1). The spine concept SHOULD propagate consistently into the location/confirmation axes. Do NOT introduce a looser BOS condition for axes 4/5. This is BINDING for the Iter-3 sweep.**

Reasoning (trading-logic, verified against the engines at source):
1. **The two awards are SEMANTICALLY a structure-shift confirmation, so they must share the spine's definition.** Axis 4 is literally "sweep + inducement: the structure shift confirms a sweep was reclaimed" (scorer:141). Axis 5 awards the confirmation point when "a confirming structure shift is present" (scorer:147-149). A *stale* (e.g. 30+ bars old) or *wrong-direction* BOS is NOT a confirmation of THIS signal's sweep/reclaim — awarding +1/+1 off it would be rewarding a structure event that the freshness+direction gate just declared irrelevant. Using a looser condition for the points than for the gate would re-introduce exactly the stale-latch inflation Phase 2.2 exists to remove, just one layer down in the score. **One definition of "fresh aligned structure shift", used consistently** — no second, weaker definition.
2. **The blast radius is small and self-limiting, because every current engine sets `requiresConfirmation=false` and supplies its own `engine_confluence>0` spine.** Verified: CExpansionEngine:514/529, CReversalSweepEngine:203, CRangeReversionEngine:128, CTrendContinuationEngine:152/416/440 all set `requiresConfirmation=false`. Consequences:
   - **Axis 5 is UNAFFECTED by the tightening for every engine signal.** Line 150 is `if(!signal.requiresConfirmation || structure_shift)`; with `requiresConfirmation=false`, `!false==true` short-circuits → axis 5 awards +1 regardless of `structure_shift`. The OR is the looser fallback already. So mt5's worry about axis 5 does not bite current engines at all; it only matters for a hypothetical future engine that opts INTO confirmation, where withholding the point on a stale/contra BOS is the CORRECT conservative behavior anyway.
   - **Axis 4 is the only axis the tightening actually affects:** a stale/contra BOS withholds its +1. But the engine's `engine_confluence>0` still clears the L3 hard gate (`engine_spine`, scorer:102-103), so the signal is NOT killed — it merely scores 1 point lower on the location/inducement axis, which is the honest outcome (no fresh aligned shift → no inducement-confirmation credit). This is a tier *de-rating*, never a wrongful rejection.
3. **Net effect when engines turn ON (Iter 3): the gate makes scoring more honest without starving the engines.** It cannot zero an engine (the `engine_confluence>0` spine path is independent). It can only shave up to ~1 point off setups whose structure shift is stale or contra-directional — exactly the setups that should rank lower. A looser axis-4/5 condition would defeat the purpose.

**Iter-3 verification items recorded (must be re-checked with the router ON, NOT blocking 2.2 since dormant):**

If the latched BOS is OLDER than the latched CHoCH, the scorer reads the BOS *type* against the CHoCH *time* — i.e. a stale BOS could be judged "fresh" on a newer CHoCH's timestamp, or a fresh CHoCH's direction is ignored in favor of an older BOS.
