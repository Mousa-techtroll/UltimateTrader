What a professional would not do

The five decision mistakes

These are the branches where the EA's choice diverges from how a disciplined gold trader would act. Each is anchored to the deciding node in the flow below.

N27 + N4/N6Trades blind into news

The day-type is classified (N6) but the router that would flatten on a tier-1 release (N4) is dormant — so the EA holds and opens through CPI / FOMC / NFP with no event veto. A discretionary gold trader flattens or stands aside around tier-1 data.

X4 + N16No room-to-target, and it chases

The reward-room / obstacle gate (X4) is off by default, so it buys straight into the previous-day high / overhead supply; and entry is market-on-close (N16) with no single-candle extension cap — it will chase an extended bar into resistance with nowhere to go.

N27No revenge / flip-flop cooldown

Signal generation runs with nothing throttling re-entry right after a stop-out — a long can lose and an opposite short can fire on the next bar during chop, unguarded.

N32Slots capped by a count, not exposure

Five "independent" longs are really one correlated gold bet bounded only by a position count (5). Only the 5% portfolio exposure cap (X11) stands between that count and a true overbet — there is no per-direction / per-family budget.

F2File/CSV signals enter under-vetted

External signals are permitted on only count + halt + CanTrade — they bypass shock, session-quality, spread, thrash, the entire validator / SMC / quality cascade, and the minimum R:R gate. An external signal enters far less vetted than an internal one.
